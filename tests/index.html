Stygi 3 years ago#1
I first played this game 9 years ago (Special Edition, HD Collection) on Normal/Gold mode. Didn't go any farther because I was more of a Bayonetta/MGR fan at the time. Still am a fan of them, but I appreciate DMC equally now. I've played 1, 3, and 4. 5 looks shiny but I have not felt compelled to play a new game in a long time.

Now I'm playing on Hard/Yellow, since I read that's how the original North American release that everyone complained about was. It's been long enough that I forgot almost everything about it. Up to Agni and Rudra now. It's hard, but there isn't any unreactable bulls***, and all attacks can be canceled out of, so I'm enjoying it. I can never do strict timing stuff like perfect Royal Guards or jump canceling consistently though, so I mostly play without them.

I also love the music, and the overall aesthetics of all the games. All the DMC games have this delightfully obnoxious late 90s-mid 00s style rock music.

I was wondering if anyone is still hanging around here. I see that there are some posts from 1-2 months ago, so I was wondering if any of you guys are still playing DMC, and if not, when did you play it last, and which game?

Stygi (Topic Creator)3 years ago#6
Stygi posted...
all attacks can be canceled out of

I'm not sure why it took me so long to notice this, but this is apparently not true. The attacks have varying degrees of cancelability; in general, it seems that some frames of the attack animation need to complete before you can jump or do anything else. With some weapons, like Cerberus' basic attacks, the frames needed to complete are either nonexistent or so few that it effectively feels like you can cancel whenever. I guess I didn't notice before because I was tanking hits like Rocky on Normal mode, and there's nothing as egregious as waiting 1-2 seconds for a greatsword swing to complete in Monster Hunter.

I had a much easier time with Agni and Rudra after having this realization, LOL.
